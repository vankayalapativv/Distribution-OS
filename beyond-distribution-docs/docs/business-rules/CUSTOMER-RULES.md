# Customer Rules

Configurable customer rules include:

- require legal name
- require phone number
- require territory
- require executive assignment
- require geo-location
- require signboard photo
- GST mandatory for GST billing customer
- new customer requires approval
- customer grade assignment requires first order or approval
- block inactive customer from new order

Recommended rule entities:

- rule_definitions
- rule_conditions
- rule_actions
- rule_executions
